*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the International Labour Organisation Conventions Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,

“ILO” means the International Labour Organisation;

“ILO Conventions” means the Conventions of the ILO, the full texts of which are published from time to time by the International Labour Office;

“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for Labour.

ILO Conventions to have the force of law in Belize. Schedule.

**3.** (1) Notwithstanding any other law, but subject to the provisions of this Act, the ILO Conventions ratified by Belize as listed in the Schedule hereto, shall have the force of law in Belize.

(2) The Minister may from time to time by Order published in the Gazette amend the Schedule.

*Saving.*

**4.** This Act shall be read and construed as being in addition to, and not in derogation of, the provisions of the Labour Act,

*THE*

### SUBSTANTIVE LAWS OF BELIZE

Cap. 297, but where there is a conflict between the provisions of this Act and the Labour Act, Cap. 297, the provisions of this Act shall prevail.

*Regulations.*

**5.** (1) The Minister may make regulations for the better carrying out of the objects and purposes of this Act and the ILO Conventions, ratified by Belize.

(2) All regulations made by the Minister under subsection

(1) of this section shall be subject to negative resolution.
