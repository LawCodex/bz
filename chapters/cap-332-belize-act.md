## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS Interpretation.

CAP. 260, [1st June, 1973] R. E. 1980-1990.

13 of 1973.

This Act may be cited as the Belize Act.

On and after 1st June, 1973 the name of the Territory which immediately before the said 1st June constitutes the Colony of British Honduras shall be Belize.

*Interpretation.*

**3.** On and after the said 1st June any reference in any Ordinance, Act, Statutory Instrument or public document in whatever words to British Honduras shall be construed in relation to any period after that date as a reference to Belize.
