Establishment, Objectives and Functions of the BELTRAIDE

*Establishment of the BELTRAIDE.*

**3.** (1) A body to be known as the Belize Trade and Investment Development Service is hereby established as an autonomous institution governed by the provisions of this Act.

(2) The BELTRAIDE may exercise any of the functions entrusted to it by or in accordance with the provisions of this Act or any regulations made thereunder, and may execute any other duties incidental or ancillary to, or consequential upon, the performance of its functions.

*Board to be protected by Public Authorities Protection Act. CAP. 31.*

**11.** The Board shall be entitled to the same protection under the Public Authorities Protection Act, as if it were included in the definition of “Public Authority” given in section 2, and the place at which the Board shall hold its meetings shall be deemed to be the “abode” of the Board within the meaning of that Act.

*Delegation of powers. 2 of 2000.*

**12.** (1) Subject to the provisions of this Act, the BELTRAIDE may delegate to any member of the Board or to any committee established pursuant to this Act, the power and authority to carry out on its behalf such duties as the Board of Directors of the BELTRAIDE may from time to time determine.

(2) It shall be lawful for the BELTRAIDE to establish committees for the full and effectual discharge of its duties and authorise such committees to analyse, investigate, research and report back to the BELTRAIDE on any matter which the Board of Directors of the BELTRAIDE feels should be analysed, investigated, researched and reported back by any committee.

(3) Members of any committee established pursuant to this section may either be members of the Board of Directors of the

BELTRAIDE or such other technical persons with expertise in the area to be analysed, investigated and researched as the BELTRAIDE may determine from time to time.

*Protection of Directors.*

**13.** No director shall be personally liable for any act or omission of the BELTRAIDE or of the Board, or of any director, or officer of the BELTRAIDE, if the act or omission is made in good faith in the course of the operations of the BELTRAIDE, or the Board.

*Rules of the BELTRAIDE.*

**14.** (1) The Board may from time to time with the approval of the Minister, make rules of the BELTRAIDE, not inconsistent with this Act, for all or any of the following purposes–

(a) regulating the proceedings of the Board;

(b) providing for the custody of the property of the BELTRAIDE, and the custody and use of the common seal of the BELTRAIDE;

(c) regulating the terms and conditions of employment of persons appointed under section 16;

(d) preparing quarterly and annual reviews of the BELTRAIDE; and

(e) providing for such other matters as may be necessary or expedient for the better carrying out of the functions of the BELTRAIDE.

(2) Notice in writing of every proposed resolution to make rules under this section or for the amendment or revocation of any rule so made, (including a copy of the proposed rules, amendment or revocation) shall be given to every director not less than twenty-one days before the meeting at which the proposal is to be moved, but any inadvertent failure to comply with this sub-section shall not invalidate the making, amendment, or revocation of any rule at that meeting.

*Directions by Minister.*

The Minister may give written directions of a general nature pertaining to the policy to be followed by the Board in the performance of its functions and the attainment of its objectives and the Board shall comply with such directions.

*Appointment of staff. 10 of 2011. 50 of 2017.*

**16.** (1) The Executive Director shall employ such persons as officers and employees of the BELTRAIDE as may be necessary for the proper performance of the functions of the BELTRAIDE.

(2) The employment, including the terms and conditions, discipline and dismissal of members of the staff of the BELTRAIDE shall be in accordance with the policies and guidelines established by the BELTRAIDE for this purpose and all such employment shall be subject to the approval of the Board.

*Duties of Executive Director. 2 of 2000. 10 of 2011. 50 of 2017.*

**17.** (1) The Executive Director shall, subject to the general policy directions of the Board, be responsible for the management and administration of the BELTRAIDE including the employment of the staff of the BELTRAIDE pursuant to section 16.

(2) The Executive Director shall have authority to sign jointly with any other director duly authorised by the Board for that purpose, any reports, balance sheets and other financial statements.

*10 of 2011. 50 of 2017.*

(3) The Executive Director shall assist the Chairman in the discharge of his functions and duties under this Act, and it shall be lawful for the Chairman to delegate to the Executive Director all or part of such functions and duties.

*10 of 2011. 50 of 2017*

(4) The Executive Director under section 16 (a), shall be selected from among directors of the functional units of the BELTRAIDE.

Financial Provisions, Accounts and Reports

*Revenue of the BELTRAIDE.*

**18.** The revenues of the BELTRAIDE shall consist of the following–

(a) revenues allocated from the Consolidated Revenue Fund;

(b) revenues obtained from any fees charged by the BELTRAIDE for the provision of its services;

(c) grants from international, regional or local funding agencies;

(d) any other monies lawfully contributed, donated, or bequeathed to the BELTRAIDE from any other source.

*Fees for services.*

*10 of 2011.*

**19.** The BELTRAIDE may, in connection with any service it is requested to provide by virtue of the provisions of this Act, charge fees from, or make other financial arrangements with, the person requesting the services, or may earn fees or income from other activities using any expertise, patents, abilities, or special designations controlled by the BELTRAIDE.

*Expenses of the BELTRAIDE. 10 of 2011.*

**20.** The expenses of the BELTRAIDE, including the remuneration of directors and staff, shall be paid out of its annual approved budget and any income earned under section

19.

*Submission of budget estimates.*

**21.** (1) The BELTRAIDE shall, in such form and by such dates as may be prescribed by the Financial Secretary, prepare and submit to the Minister of Finance, through the Minister, estimates of revenue and expenditure (including any supplementary estimates) for each financial year and the Minister of Finance shall present the said estimates to the

National Assembly with such amendments, if any, as he may consider necessary.

(2) Except with the approval of the Minister of Finance, no further sum shall be expended in any financial year other than that provided in the budget relating to such financial year.

*Accounts and audits.*

**22.** (1) The BELTRAIDE shall keep accounts and other records in relation to its business and shall prepare annually a statement of accounts in accordance with the generally accepted accounting principles.

(2) The BELTRAIDE shall, with the approval of the Minister of Finance, open and maintain, at such bank or banks as it may from time to time determine, accounts for the funds of the BELTRAIDE.

(3) All accounts of the BELTRAIDE shall be audited annually by an auditor appointed by the Board, with the approval of the Minister of Finance.

*Annual report.*

**23.** (1) Within four months after the end of each financial year, the BELTRAIDE shall cause to be made and shall submit to the Minister–

(a) a statement of its accounts audited in accordance with section 22(3); and

(b) a report dealing generally with the proceedings, policies and performances of the BELTRAIDE during that financial year.

(2) The Minister shall cause a copy of such report, within three months of receiving it, together with the annual statement of accounts and the auditor’s report thereon, to be tabled in the National Assembly.

Miscellaneous

*Validity of Board’s acts.*

**24.** No acts done or proceedings taken under this Act shall be questioned or invalidated on the grounds–

(a) of the existence of any vacancy in the membership, or of any defect in the constitution, of the Board; or

(b) of any omission, defect or irregularity.

*Exemption from income tax, property tax, stamp duty and import duty.*

**25.** (1) The BELTRAIDE shall be exempt from the payment of income tax, property tax, import duty and any other tax or impost levied by the Government or a local authority.

(2) All instruments executed by or on behalf of the BELTRAIDE shall be exempt from stamp duty.

*Regulations.*

**26.** (1) The Board may, with the approval of the Minister, make regulations generally for the better carrying out of the objects and purposes of this Act.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of sub-section (1), such regulations may provide for all or any of the following–

(a) the various services provided by the BELTRAIDE; and

(b) the level of fees charged by the BELTRAIDE when requested to perform a service by a specific person;

*50 of 2017.*

(c) the promotion, development, administration of micro, small and medium-sized enterprises.
