## PART I Preliminary

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Refrigeration Technicians (Licensing) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,

“Association” means the Association of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians of Belize established under section 3;

“Certificate of Registration” means a certificate issued pursuant to section 17 of this Act;

“Constitution” means the Constitution of the Association prescribed in the Schedule to this Act;

“Council” means the Council of the Association of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians of Belize established under section 5 of this Act;

“licence” means a licence issued in accordance with section 17 of this Act;

“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for the Environment;

“practice” means the installation, repairs or servicing of refrigeration or air conditioning equipment;

“Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician” means a person who is registered and licensed to practise in the refrigeration and air conditioning sector;

“Register” means the Register required to be kept pursuant to section 11 of this Act.

*Register.*

**11.** (1) The Council shall keep a Register of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians in which it shall cause to be entered the name of every person required by section 12 or entitled under section 16 of this Act, to be registered as a Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician together with the following particulars in relation to each such person,

(a) his full address;

(b) the date of his registration, or in the case of section 12 of this Act, the date of his qualification;

(c) the description of his qualifications.

(2) The Register shall, at all reasonable times, be open to inspection at the Council’s office without payment of a fee.

(3) A person whose name is included in the Register shall as and when any change occurs in his qualifications or address, inform the Council of such change.

(4) The Council, if satisfied about the change in qualifications or address, shall make the necessary changes in the Register.

(5) A copy of the Register duly certified by the Secretary to the Counci1 to be a correct copy shall be prima facie evidence that any person named in the Register is registered as a Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

*Licensing of existing Refrigeration Technicians.*

**12.** (1) Within three months after the commencement of this Act, the Council shall, without payment of a fee, cause to be entered in the Register, the particulars specified in section 11(1) (a), (b) and (c) of this Act, of persons who can prove that immediately before such commencement, they were practising as Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians in Belize; and upon such entry, the persons shall be deemed to be Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians within the meaning of this Act and shall be licensed accordingly.

*Licence and Certificate of Registration.*

**17.** (1) If the Admissions Committee recommends to the Counci1 that an applicant is fit and qualified to be licensed as a Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician, the Council shall, after receiving the prescribed licensing fee from the applicant, issue him with a License in the prescribed form.

(2) The Council shall, after issuing a license, authorize the Secretary in writing to enter the name of the applicant in the Register, and the Secretary shall as soon as possible enter the name of the applicant in the Register and furnish the applicant with a certificate of registration in the prescribed form for refrigeration and air conditioning.

(3) A license shall expire on the 3lst of December in each year and must be renewed in accordance with section 13 of this Act.

(4) Subject to this Act and any regulations made there under, the Council may attach to the issue or renewal of a licence such conditions as may have been approved by a two-thirds majority of the membership of the Association at an Annual or Special General Meeting.

(5) In the event of a breach of any of the conditions of the licence, the Council may, after giving the licence-holder a reasonable opportunity to make representations in that behalf, revoke or suspend the licence.

*Entitlement to practice.*

Every person licensed as a Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician under this Act shall be entitled to practise refrigeration and air conditioning profession in Belize and to demand and charge reasonable fees for services rendered by him as a Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician.

## PART IV

Miscellaneous

*Prohibition on practice without a licence.*

**19.** (1) No person shall engage in the practice of refrigeration and air conditioning or hold himself out as a Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician unless that person is licensed under this Act.

(2) For the purpose of this section, proof of the performance of one act in the practice of refrigeration and air conditioning on one occasion is sufficient to establish that the person was engaging in the practice of refrigeration and air conditioning.

(3) A person who contravenes this section commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a period of one year or to both fine and period of imprisonment.

*Association may institute legal proceedings.*

**20.** The Association may in its own name, by any officer of the Council or any other person duly authorized in that behalf under the hand of its President, institute, carry on, prosecute and defend any actions, complaint, information or proceedings whatever, and every court shall take judicial notice of the signature of the President of the Council to any such authorization.

*Use of titles.*

**21.** A licensed person shall be entitled to take and use the name or title of “Licensed Refrigeration and Air Conditioning

Technician,” but no person shall practice under any name, title or style containing the words

“Licensed Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician” unless he is licensed in accordance with the provisions of this Act, and any unlicensed person who so practices or wilfully pretends to be a licensed Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician, or takes or uses the name or title of a licensed Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician or any name, title, style or description implying that he is a licensed person, is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding one year, or to both such fine and period of imprisonment.

*Removal of name from Register for failure to notify present address.*

**22.** For the purpose of maintaining the Register the Council may at any time by notice in writing addressed to the last known address of any Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician as reflected in the Register, enquire if such a professional has changed his regular business address, and if no answer is received within six months from the sending of such notice, the Council shall again send to the Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician a further notice by registered post, and if no answer is received within three months from the sending of the further notice, it shall be lawful for the Council to cause the Secretary to remove the name of the Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician from the Register.

*Offences and penalties.*

**23.** (1) A person who procures or attempts to procure a licence as Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician for himself or for any other person by making or producing or causing to be made or produced any false or fraudulent representation or declaration, either orally or in writing, commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding one year or to both such fine and term of imprisonment.

(2) Any person who, during a period when his licence as a Refrigeration or Air Conditioning Technician under this Act is suspended, or who, not being licensed as a Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician, practices refrigeration and air conditioning services or any branch of this profession, or acts as a partner of any Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technician commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding one year, or to both such fine and term of imprisonment.

*General penalty provision.*

**24.** A person who contravenes any provision of this Act for which no other penalty is provided is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding five thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding one year or to both such fine and period of imprisonment.

*Recovery of penalties.*

**25.** All penalties and fees imposed by or pursuant to this Act may be recovered summarily.

*Time limit for proceedings.*

**26.** Any proceedings under this Act may be instituted within twelve months after the offence is committed, or within twelve months after the discovery of the offence, whichever is the later period.

*Association to be governed by its Constitution*

**27.** (1) The Association shall be governed by its Constitution set out in the Schedule to this Act which Schedule shall be read and construed as an integral part of this Act.

(2) The Association may, from time to time by a two-thirds majority of its membership, amend the Schedule by notice published in the Gazette and in at least one national newspaper.

## SCHEDULE REFRIGERATION TECHNICIANS (LICENSING) ACT. Constitution of the Association of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians of Belize

[Section 27] Article I Name of Association The name of the association shall be the “Association of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians of Belize.” Article II Objectives of the Association The objects and purposes for which the Association of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians of Belize (hereinafter called “the Association”) is hereby constituted are, (i) to promote, encourage and protect the general advancement of the profession of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians Professions (hereinafter called “the Professions”); (ii) to facilitate the exchange of information and ideas among the members of the Association;

(iii) to provide a means of evaluating the development of professional skills

among members of the professions and to provide professional status to those members of the professions who have exemplified themselves as worthy of such status in accordance with the terms and conditions laid down by this Constitution and its By-laws;

(iv) to regulate the conduct of members of the Association such as to encourage the application of the highest standards of excellence in the performance of services and in the ethics of the professions;

(v) to implement standards in the practice of the professions and instill a conscious recognition of the professions’ obligation to society and the environment;

(vi) to seek, for the protection and benefit of society and the environment, legislation for mandatory registration of Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians to practice in Belize.

Article III

Membership

**1.** Subject to the provisions of the Act, membership in the Association shall be open to qualified Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Technicians. The membership of the Association shall consist of the following classes,

(a) Corporate/ Professional Members;

(b) Individual Members;

(c) Associate Members;

(d) Honorary Members;

(e) Student Members.

**2.** Corporate/Professional Members

Every candidate for election or transfer to the class of Corporate Member shall be a person who in the opinion of the Council has by his achievements acquired an established reputation and whose admission as a Corporate Member will be in the interest of the Association.

In addition he shall either,

(a) (i) hold a degree, diploma or an equivalent award in Refrigeration and Air Conditioning or an allied Profession, from a University or place of learning at which the syllabus for the particular field is approved by the Council; and

(ii) have at least four years practical experience after graduation of a nature acceptable to the Council, under the guidance and supervision of a Corporate Member, or a Corporate Member of a like society of international repute, who is prepared to vouch for the experience gained; or

(b) be a Corporate Member of a Society similarly constituted which is approved by the Council and has had practical experience in Belize for a period of not less than ten years.

**3.** Individual Members

Every candidate for election to the class of Individual Member shall be a person who, in the opinion of the Council, is qualified either by experience or education and who is directly involved in the practice of refrigeration or air conditioning trade in Belize. Preference will be given to a person who holds a degree, diploma or equivalent award in Refrigeration and Air Conditioning or an allied Profession from a University or place of learning at which the syllabus for the particular field is approved by the Council.

**4.** Associate Members

Every candidate for election to the class of Associate Member shall be a person whose admission will, in the opinion of the Council, be in the interest of the Association. In addition, he shall hold a degree, diploma or equivalent award in Refrigeration and Air Conditioning or an allied Profession from a University or place of leaning at which the syllabus for the particular field is approved by the Council.

**5.** Honorary Members

Honorary Members shall comprise every person who has been elected into the class of Honorary Member so long as his name is on the Register as such. They shall be either distinguished persons who from their position have been or are able to render assistance in the execution of public works, or persons eminent in science and experienced in pursuits connected with the professions of refrigeration and air conditioning but who are not engaged in the practice of the professions.

Student Members

Every candidate for the election to the class of Student Member shall be a person who, at the time of his application, shall be actively pursuing a course of studies leading to an academic qualification acceptable to the Council. Student Members involved in training to achieve experience shall be under the supervision of a qualified license technician for a period not exceeding five years. No person shall remain in this grade for more than 5 years.

Article IV

Admission, Expulsion and Retirement

**1.** Candidates shall be admitted to membership in the Association only by the Council. A candidate elected shall be admitted as a Corporate / Professional Member, Individual Member, Associate Member, Honorary Member or Student Member, as the case may be, on payment of such entrance fee or instalment and annual subscription as the By-laws may prescribe.

Should payment of such dues not be made within the period of three calendar months after the date of notification to the candidate of his election, such election may be declared null and void by the Council and the candidate shall be notified accordingly.

**2.** The Council in accordance with the By-laws may expel any member who shall have wilfully acted in contravention of the By-laws and/or Standards or Code of Professional Conduct of the Association, or who, in the opinion of the Council, shall have been guilty of such conduct as shall render him unfit to continue to belong to the Association, and he shall thereupon cease to be a Member (of any class) of the Association.

Any member of any class may retire from the Association by sending his resignation in writing to the Secretary, after payment of all subscriptions due from him, including for that current year.

Article V

Fees and Dues

A license fee in an amount determined by the Council for the class of membership to which the candidate is elected shall be due and payable as of the date of election to that class. The schedules of fees and annual dues shall be established, and when established may be modified by a two-thirds majority of the Council. The schedule of fees and dues shall be set forth in the By-laws.

Article VI

Nomination and Election of Officers

**1.** The Council shall consist of the President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer and three other members. All members of the Council shall be Corporate/ Professional or Individual Members.

**2.** The term of office of all elected officers shall be two years and the conditions of eligibility for re-election to the same office or any other office shall be as specified in the By-laws. A term of office shall begin at the close of the Annual General Meeting at which the Officer’s election is announced and shall continue until a successor is elected.

**3.** Vacancies in the offices of President, Vice-President, Secretary, Treasurer and Members of Council shall, subject to the provisions of the By-laws, be filled every two years by a ballot of members of the Association.

In the case of disability of any member of the Council, or neglect in performance of his duties, the Council, on the approval of the membership of the Association, shall have power to declare that office vacant.

Article VII

Management

**1.** The Council shall direct and manage the property and affairs of the Association in accordance with the Regulations and the By-laws of the Association and may exercise all such powers of the Association except those required to be exercised by the Association in meeting.

**2.** The day-to-day management of the affairs of the Association shall be exercised by the Secretary acting as general manager under authority of the Council.

Article VIII

Meetings

**1.** General Meetings of the Association shall be held at such places and at such times as the Council may determine.

**2.** The meetings of the Association shall be as follows,

(a) Ordinary Meetings for the reading and discussion of papers, for lectures and discussions, and for visits to works of interest;

(b) The Annual General Meetings;

(c) Special General Meetings.

**3.** All members shall be entitled to vote at the meetings specified in paragraph 2 of this Article, and each voting member shall have one vote.

**4.** In addition to the Ordinary Meetings specified by paragraph 2 (a), the Council may at their discretion arrange for other meetings to be held for the dissemination of knowledge by means of lectures or cinematograph, slide show or other pictorial representations, or for the discussion of papers; and the Council shall determine the conditions of admission to such meetings, and the manner in which they shall be conducted.

**5.** The Ordinary Meetings of the Association shall be conducted as prescribed by the Council from time to time and the Council shall determine the conditions of admission to such meetings.

**6.** At the Ordinary Meetings no question shall be discussed or motion made, relating to the direction and management of the Association.

**7.** Every member of any class shall have the privilege of introducing one or more visitors at each Ordinary Meetings of the Association, by writing his or their names in the book provided for that purpose.

**8.** The Annual General Meeting shall be held in the month of December or at such earlier time as the Council may determine. The business of the Annual General Meeting shall be to receive and consider the Report of the Council and the Accounts of the Association for the past year; to elect officers, and (with the approval of the Council) to transact any other business of which notice in writing shall have been given to the Secretary at least seven days before such meeting.

A special General Meeting may be called at any time by the Council for any specific purpose relating to the direction and management of the affairs of the Association, or for amending the Constitution or any By-Laws of the Association, and the Council shall at all times call such meeting on a requisition of five members specifying the nature of the business to be transacted.

Article IX

Organization

**1.** In pursuance of the objects of the Association, the Council may at their discretion set up one or more sub-committees of the Association as they may specify, for the holding of meetings, the reading of papers and for the discussion of subjects or for other activities falling within the purview of the Association, and the Council may dissolve any such sub-committee at any time.

**2.** The Council may form such Branches and/or Divisions as may seem to them necessary in order to meet the needs of groups of members. The continuance of such Branches and/or Divisions and the terms of their relationship with the Council, and the rules under which they operate, shall be as the Counci1 may determine under the By-laws.

Article X

Amendments

**1.** A proposed amendment to the Constitution shall be submitted to the Secretary in written petitions signed by not less than five members of the Association. Counterparts of petitions may be signed and presented with the same effect as if the signature were on one petition.

Petitions for a proposed amendment shall be presented to the Secretary not less than twenty-eight days in advance of the date of the Annual General Meeting or Special General Meeting. A proposed amendment in accordance with the intent of the petitions shall be drafted by the Secretary and sent by letter to all the members of the Association at least fourteen days in advance of the Annual General Meeting or Special General Meeting. The proposed amendment shall be presented for discussion at the Annual General Meeting or Special General Meeting and may be amended in any manner pertinent to the petitions by two- thirds vote.

Provided that, if the meeting shall so decide by majority vote, it may defer the proposed amendment to a committee for further consideration and a report at the next Annual General Meeting or Special General Meeting.

**3.** In a similar manner, amendments to the By-laws of the Association may be proposed but only at the Special General Meeting convened for that purpose. Such amendments shall only be regarded as approved by the Association, if two-thirds or more of the members present in person or by proxy at such Special General Meeting vote in favour of such amendment.

Article XI

Incorporation of Kindred Societies

The Council may, upon receipt of a request to the effect, from any Society with objects kindred to those of the Association, arrange for the union, alliance or incorporation of such Society with the Association and may also, remit or reduce the entrance fees of the members of such Society at the time of their union, alliance or incorporation,

Provided that no such union, alliance or incorporation shall be effective unless it is sanctioned by the membership of the Association at a Special General Meeting.
