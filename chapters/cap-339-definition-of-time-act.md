*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Definition of Time Act.

*Standard Time.*

**2.** The Standard Time to be observed throughout Belize for all purposes whatever, save as hereinafter provided, shall be the mean time of Longitude ninety degrees West, which is seven minutes, seventeen seconds and twenty-seven hundredths of a second later than the mean time at Belize City and six hours later than Greenwich mean time.

*Minister may vary local time. 40 of 1963. 22 of 1973.*

**3.** The Minister responsible for home affairs may from time to time by Order published in the Gazette prescribe that the local time in Belize shall vary from Standard Time as fixed in section 2 of this Act, by such lengths of time and for such periods as he shall therein specify.

*Reference to time occurring in enactments, etc.*

*22 of 1973.*

**4.** Subject to the provisions of section 5, whenever any reference to a point of time occurs in any enactment, Act, proclamation, Order in Council, Order, regulation rule, by-law, deed, notice or other document whatever, unless it is otherwise specifically stated, the time referred to shall be deemed to be the time fixed by section 2 or such other time as the Minister responsible for home affairs may under section 3 have ordered.

*Reservation for astronomy, meteorology or navigation.*

**5.** Nothing in this Act shall affect the use of Greenwich mean time for purpose of astronomy, meteorology or navigation or affect the construction of any document mentioning or
