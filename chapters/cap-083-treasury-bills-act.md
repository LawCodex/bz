*Issue of Treasury Bills and Treasury Notes to be sufficient evidence that authority of Minister given. 15 of 1993.*

**8.** The issue of Treasury Bills and Treasury Notes by the Financial Secretary in the form prescribed by rules made under section 9 shall be sufficient evidence on behalf of any holder thereof that the authority of the Minister had been given for such issue in accordance with section 3 and that such issue has been made in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

*Power to make rules. 15 of 1993.*

**9.** The Minister may make rules to prescribe or provide for–

(a) the preparation, form, mode of issue, mode of payment and cancellation of Treasury Bills and Treasury Notes;

(b) the issue of a new Bill or Note in lieu of one which has been defaced, lost or destroyed; or

(c) such other matters as may seem necessary to him for the purposes of giving effect to the provisions of this Act.
