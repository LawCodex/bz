### CHAPTER 24:04

### INTERNATIONAL MARITIME ORGANISATION CONVENTIONS

[31st December, 2008] 17 of 2008.

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the International Maritime Organisation Conventions Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires;

“IMO” means the International Maritime Organisation;

“IMO Conventions and Protocols” means the Conventions and Protocols of the International Maritime Organisation, the full texts of which are published from time to time by the IMO;

“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for the International Merchant Marine Registry of Belize (IMMARBE);

“Registrar” means the Registrar of Merchant Shipping appointed by the Minister under section 4 of the Registration of Merchant Ships Act, as amended;

“Senior Deputy Registrar” means Senior Deputy Registrar of Merchant Shipping appointed by the Registrar under section 4(3) of the Registration of Merchant Ships Act, Cap. 236, as amended.

*IMO Convention to have the force of law in Belize Schedule*

**3.** (1) Notwithstanding any other law, but subject to the provisions of this Act, the IMO Conventions and Protocols acceded to by Belize, as listed in the Schedule hereto, shall have the force of law in Belize.

*THE*

### SUBSTANTIVE LAWS OF BELIZE

(2) The Minister may from time to time by Order published in the Gazette amend the Schedule.

*Relationship with other laws.*

**4.** This Act shall be read and construed as being in addition to, and not in derogation of, the provisions of the Registration of Merchant Ships Act, Cap. 236 and any Regulations issued thereunder, but where there is a conflict between the provisions of this Act and the Registration of Merchant Ships Act, Cap. 236, the provisions of this Act shall prevail.

*Regulations.*

**5.** (1) The Minister may make regulations for the better carrying out of the objects and purposes of this Act and the IMO Conventions and Protocols acceded to by Belize.

(2) All regulations made by the Minister under subsection

(1) of this section shall be subject to negative resolution.

*Notices and Circular letters.*

**6.** Subject to this Act and the Regulations made by the Minister under section 5, the Registrar, or the Senior Deputy Registrar with the approval of the Registrar may issue notices, circular letters and directives to shipowners and other persons concerned, for the better carrying out of the objects and purposes of this Act and the IMO Conventions and Protocols acceded to by Belize.

*EDITION 2020*
