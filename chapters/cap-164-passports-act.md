## PART I

Preliminary

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Passports Act.

*Form of application, and fees. 14 of 1968. 13 of 1986. S.I. 81 of 1981.*

**2.** The form of application for and the particulars to be furnished in respect of a Belize passport issued to any person, and any fee to be paid therefore, may from time to time be prescribed by the Minister by regulations issued under the provisions of this Act.
