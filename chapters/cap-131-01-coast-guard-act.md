Establishment, Duties, Functions and Command etc. of the Coast Guard

*Establishment of the Coast Guard. 3 of 2016.*

**3.** (1) There shall be and is hereby established in and for Belize a Coast Guard (hereinafter referred to as “the Coast Guard”) which shall be organized, trained and equipped for discharging the duties and functions specified in this Act.

*3 of 2016.*

(2) The Coast Guard shall be governed by the provisions of this Act and any Regulations made there under.

*3 of 2016.*

(3) The Minister may establish the Headquarters of the Coast Guard, and such other support units and individual operating units such as will enable the Coast Guard to fully and effectually perform the functions and discharge the duties imposed upon it by or under this Act.

*Functions and duties of the Coast Guard. 3 of 2016.*

(3) The Coast Guard shall also be comprised of such other officers as may be transferred thereto on the commencement of this Act from the Belize Defence Force, the Belize Police Department, the Customs and Excise Department, the National Fire Service, the Department of Border Management and Immigration, the Belize Port Authority or the Fisheries Department, or who may be appointed to the Coast Guard by the Security Services Commission acting pursuant to a recommendation of the Coast Guard Board established under section 8.

*3 of 2016.*

(4) Officers of the Coast Guard appointed pursuant to subsection (3), shall be in such numbers as to achieve organizational hierarchy within the Coast Guard Service and may include Commanding Officers, Group Commanders, Patrol Commanders, Captains of the Port and Boarding Officers.

*3 of 2016.*

(5) All powers, duties and functions conferred upon the Coast Guard shall be vested in the Commandant unless provided otherwise in this Act or any Regulations made thereunder, and the Commandant may, in executing such powers, duties and functions, delegate them or any of them to any of the officers appointed pursuant to sub-section (3).

*3 of 2016.*

(6) The Minister may make Regulations setting out the hierarchical structure of the Coast Guard, and providing therein the duties of any of the officers referred to in sub-section (4).

*Overseas training of members. 3 of 2016.*

**6.** (1) The Security Services Commission may permit any member of the Coast Guard to proceed to a place or places outside Belize for the purpose of undergoing training or instruction, or for duty or employment.

*3 of 2016.*

(2) The Security Services Commission may place any member of the Coast Guard at the disposal of the coast guard authorities of any other country or territory for the purpose of having such member attached to the coast guard authorities of such country or territory for duty or training or both.

(3) The Minister may enter into an agreement with the coast guard authorities of any other country or territory to permit a member of the coast guard service of that country to be attached to the coast guard authorities of Belize for training or employment.

*Power of command of members if cooperating with other security forces. 3 of 2016.*

**7.** (1) As a separate branch of the security forces of Belize, the Coast Guard may, with the approval of the Minister, and with or without reimbursement for the cost thereof, send personnel, officers and members to any other security forces of Belize, or receives personnel, officers and members from any other security force of Belize, for the purpose of joint training or instruct ion operations.

*3 of 2016.*

(2) Where personnel, officers and members of the Coast Guard are sent for training or instruction pursuant to sub-section (1), they shall be subject to the general rules, directives and other laws governing the receiving security force.

*3 of 2016.*

(3) From time to time, as the Minister may direct, the Coast Guard shall conduct joint operations with other security forces of Belize or of other countries for the purpose of facilitating a cooperative and efficient approach to national, regional or international defence and security.

*3 of 2016.*

(4) In so far as powers of command depend on rank, any member of a security force who is individually acting with, or is a member of a force which is jointly acting with, the Coast Guard shall have the same powers of command as an officer of the Coast Guard of the same or similar rank.

*3 of 2016.*

(5) If the whole Coast Guard is for any reason required to act with any other security force of Belize, the Governor-General may place the Coast Guard or such part thereof as he may determine from time to time under the command of the officer commanding such other security force.

*3 of 2016.*

(6) Where any unit of the Coast Guard is acting in cooperation with any other security force in Belize, the Officer

in command of the Coast Guard Unit may, in agreement with the commander of the other security force, define the powers of command and order of precedence of officers and other members of the Coast Guard unit and other security force.

*3 of 2016.*

*Functions of the Board. 3 of 2016.*

**9.** (1) The Board, under the general direction and authority of its Chairperson, shall be responsible for formulating the general policy direction of the Coast Guard, the training and administration of the Coast Guard, the structural organization of the Coast Guard, and may additionally perform such other functions as may from time to time be assigned to it by the Governor-General by Order published in the Gazette.

*3 of 2016.*

(2) The Board may from time to time make such recommendations to the Security Services Commission as it considers necessary regarding the appointment, disciplinary control, transfer and removal of members of the Coast Guard, other than the Commandant.

*Power to grant commissions. 3 of 2016.*

**10.** (1) The power to grant commissions in the Coast Guard shall be vested in the Security Services Commission.

*3 of 2016.*

(2) A commission may be granted either-

for a specified period of time or for an indefinite period of time;

(b) as a short term commission of three years; or

(c) after the initial commission, as a Permanent Regular Commission.

*3 of 2016. First Schedule.*

(3) The commission granted to every officer shall be signed by the Security Services Commission and shall be in the form set out in the First Schedule.

*3 of 2016.*

(4) Upon being granted a commission, every officer shall be appointed by the Security Service Commission.

*3 of 2016.*

(5) A member of the Coast Guard shall swear or affirm to the Oath of Allegiance and Office set out in the Second Schedule before assuming the duties of his office.

*Resignation and dismissal of officers. 3 of 2016.*

**11.** (1) The Security Services Commission may permit or allow an officer of the Coast Guard to resign his commission.

*3 of 2016.*

(2) The Security Services Commission may terminate the commission of an officer of the Coast Guard for inefficiency or for any other cause.

*Code of Conduct. 3 of 2016.*

**12.** Members of the Coast Guard shall conduct themselves in such a way as not–

(a) to place themselves in positions in which they have or could have a conflict of interest;

(b) to compromise the fair exercise of their public or official functions and duties;

(c) to use their office for private gain;

(d) to demean their office or position;

(e) to allow their integrity to be called into question; or

(f) to endanger or diminish respect for, or confidence in the integrity of the Government.
