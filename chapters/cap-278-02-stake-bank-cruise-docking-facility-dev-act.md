[7th February, 2014]

10 of 2014.

53 of 2017.

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Stake Bank Cruise Docking Facility Development.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires–

*CAP. 250.*

“Developer” means Stake Bank Enterprise Ltd., a company incorporated under the Companies Act, of the Laws of Belize, with its registered office at 33 New Road, Belize City;

“Facility” means the Stake Bank Cruise Docking Facility to be constructed by the Developer;

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Finance;

“Project” means all of the development and infrastructure works required for the development, financing, construction, management, operation and use of a cruise ship docking facility near the island of Stake Bank, and the development of Stake Bank for tourism purposes, including the construction of a causeway connecting the docking facility to Belize City; and

“Stake Bank Island” means the island called Stake Bank and comprising 16.085 acres and situate about 4 miles off the coast of Belize City, Belize.

*Exemption from taxes and duties. CAP. 55. No. 49/05. CAP. 48. CAP. 49. CAP. 52.*

**3.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in the Income and Business Tax Act, the General Sales Tax Act, the Customs and Excise Duties Act, the Customs Regulation Act, the Exchange Control Regulation Act and any Regulations made under the said Acts, or in any other law, regulation, rule, order, ordinance or instrument having effect as part of the laws of Belize–

(a) the Developer shall, subject to section 4, be exempt from any taxes, duties or imposts under the aforesaid Acts and other laws; and

(b) the exemptions specified in paragraph (a) shall vest in the Developer and shall have effect in Belize for a period of twenty years from the date of commencement of this Act.

*Extent of exemptions.*

**4.** The exemptions from taxes and duties granted by section 3 of this Act shall apply only to such activities of the Developer as relate to the Project, including, taxes on dividends and interest paid by the Developer.

*Port of entry and exit. CAP. 156. Cargo dues.*

**5.** The Facility shall be declared a port of entry and exit under the Immigration Act, after making necessary arrangements for customs and immigration controls.

**6.** (1) The Developer shall be entitled to collect cargo dues from all cruise ships and other vessels calling at the Facility.

*Sub Legislation CAP. 233 2003 edition p. 7.*

(2) The term ‘cargo dues’ referred to sub-section (1), shall have the meaning ascribed to it in the Belize Port Authority (Tariff) Regulations, as amended from time to time.

*EDITION 2020*

### REVISED

*Cruise Port Development fee.*

**7.** (1) There shall be paid by all cruise ship passengers entering and leaving Belize at the Facility a Cruise Port Development Fee which shall be shared between the Developer and the Government of Belize.

(2) The amount of the Cruise Ship Port Development Fee, the method of its collection and the ratio of its distribution between the Developer and the Government shall be prescribed by the Minister by regulations made under this Act.
