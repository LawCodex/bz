## PART I

Preliminary

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Protected Areas Conservation Trust Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires–

“Board” means the Board of Directors established under section 4;

“Chairperson” means the Chairperson of the Board of Directors;

*40 of 2002.*

“concession arrangements” means formal arrangements made by a legally recognised management organisation of a protected area in Belize with groups of people, or with companies or individuals, allowing such people, companies or individuals to conduct business within the protected area;

*40 of 2002.*

“cruise ship” means a sea vessel which accommodates visitors on board for lodging and is within Belize’s inland or territorial waters for less than twenty-four hours, but does not include a live a board vessel;

“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for Natural Resources;

“natural resources” means any part of the natural environment such as soil, water, minerals, flora, fauna, rocks;

*18 of 2015. CAP. 215.*

“National Protected Areas System” means the National Protected Areas System established under the National Protected Areas System Act;

*18 of 2015. CAP. 215. CAP. 330. CAP. 213.*

“protected area” means an area declared under the National Protected Areas System Act, the Forests Act or the Fisheries Act or any statutory modifications of any of those enactments for the time being in force;

*18 of 2015.*

“Minister” means the Minister for the time being responsible for protected areas;

*18 of 2015. CAP. 315.*

“non-governmental organisation” has the meaning assigned in section 3 of the Non-Governmental Organisations Act;

*40 of 2002.*

“recreation related licence fees” means fees charged to a person by the managing authority of a protected area for various recreational uses made by that person within the protected area;

“Trust” means the Protected Areas Conservation Trust established under section 3 of this Act; and

“Trust Fund” means the Protected Areas Conservation Trust Fund established under section 20 of this Act.

(5) An extraordinary vacancy shall be filled in the same manner as the appointment or election of the director vacating office, and the person filling such vacancy shall hold office for the remainder of the period which the member who has vacated his seat would have held office if he had not vacated his seat.

(3) Every person purporting to act pursuant to a delegation under this section shall, in the absence of proof to the contrary, be presumed to be acting in accordance with the terms of the delegation.

(4) Every delegation under this section shall be revocable at will, and no such delegation shall prevent the exercise of any power by the Board.
