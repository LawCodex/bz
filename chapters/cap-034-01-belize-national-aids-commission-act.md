## PART I Preliminary

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Belize National AIDS Commission Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires–

“AIDS” means the acquired immune deficiency syndrome caused by the human immunodeficiency virus;

*15 of 2008.*

“Chairperson” means the Non-Executive Chairperson of the Commission appointed by the Prime Minister under section 3;

“Commission” means the Belize National AIDS Commission established under section 3;

*15 of 2008.*

“Executive Director” means the Executive Director appointed by the Commission under section 12;

*52 of 2017.*

“Global Fund” means the Global Fund to Fight AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria;

“HIV” means the human immunodeficiency virus;

*52 of 2017.*

“HIV/AIDS-TB” means a case where TB is co-infected with HIV or AIDS; and

“TB” means tuberculosis.

## PART II

Establishment, Composition, Objectives and Functions of the Commission

*Establishment and composition of the Commission.*

**3.** (1) A body to be known as the Belize National AIDS Commission shall be and is hereby established for the purposes of this Act as an autonomous institution governed by the provisions of this Act.

(2) The Commission may exercise any of the functions entrusted to it by or in accordance with the provisions of this Act or any Regulations made thereunder, and may execute any other duties incidental to, or consequential upon, the performance of its functions.

*52 of 2017.*

(3) The Commission shall consist of the following members–

(a) a Chairperson appointed by the Prime Minister;

(b) a representative of the Ministry responsible for health;

(c) a representative of the Ministry responsible for human development;

(d) a representative of the Ministry responsible for education;

(e) the Labour Commissioner or his representative;

(f) a person nominated by the Leader of the Opposition;

(k) a person representative of key population groups recognized as particularly vulnerable to HIV infection;

(l) a representative of a national organisation representing the youth of Belize;

(m) an advocate for, or person representative of, persons who have been affected by TB;

(n) a representative from the private sector nominated by the Belize Chamber of Commerce and Industry;

(o) a representative of the Belize Council of Churches;

*52 of 2017.*

(4) The members of the Commission referred to in subsection (3) (a), (b), (c), (d), (e), (g), (h) and (r) are ex-officio members.

*52 of 2017.*

(5) The members of the Commission referred to in subsection (3) (f), (i), (l), (n), (o), (p), (q), (s), (t) and (u) of this section, may be removed from the Commission by the Prime Minister on the recommendation of the organization which, or the person who, nominated the appointment.

(6) The Prime Minister shall appoint the members of the Commission.

*52 of 2017.*

(7) The Members of the Commission shall elect a Deputy Chairperson from their number, however a member shall not be so eligible, if that member is a representative of a sub-recipient or principal recipient of the Global Fund.

*Chairperson of the Commission to preside at meetings.*

**4.** (1) The Chairperson shall, subject to sub-section (2), preside at all meetings of the Commission.

(2) If the Chairperson is for any cause unable to preside at any meetings of the Commission, the Deputy Chairperson shall preside if present and able to do so, and if the Deputy Chairperson is absent or unable to preside, the members of the Commission present shall elect one of their number to preside.

*Commission is a body corporate.*

**5.** (1) The Commission shall be a body corporate having perpetual succession and a common seal and subject to the provisions of this Act, shall have the power to acquire, hold and dispose of movable and immovable property of whatever kind and to enter into contracts and do all things necessary for the attainment of its objectives and functions.

(2) The Commission may sue and be sued in its corporate name and shall for all purposes be described by that name.

(3) Service upon the Commission of any notice, order or other document shall be executed by personally delivering the same or by sending it by registered post addressed to the Chairperson of the Commission at the main office of the Commission.

*Seal of the Commission and authentication of documents. 15 of 2008.*

**6.** (1) The seal of the Commission shall be kept in the custody of the Chairperson of the Commission or such member of the Commission as the Commission may approve and may be fixed to instruments pursuant to a decision or resolution of the Commission in the presence of the Chairperson, and one other member of the Commission duly designated by the Commission to act for that purpose.

(2) The seal of the Commission shall be authenticated by the signature of the Chairperson of the Commission and one other member of the Commission designated by the Commission to act for that purpose and such seal shall be judicially and officially noticed.

*\15. 0f 2008.*

(3) All documents, other than those required by law to be under seal, made by, and all decisions of, the Commission may be signified under the hand of the Chairperson or the Executive Director.

*Meetings of the Commission.*

**7.** (1) The Commission shall meet at such times as may be necessary or expedient for the transaction of its business, and such meetings shall be held at such places and times and on such days as the Commission may determine.

(2) The Chairperson or, in the absence of the Chairperson, the Deputy Chairperson, may at any time call a special meeting of the Commission within seven days of a written requisition for that purpose addressed to him by any ten members of the Commission.

*52 of 2017.*

(3) Nine voting members of the Commission shall constitute a quorum and in the absence of a member from any meeting, that member’s alternate is entitled to vote.

*52 of 2017.*

(3A) For the purposes of sub-section (3), a member’s alternate is a person notified in writing to the Commission by the member, or as applicable, the member’s organization specified in sub-section (3), as being qualified in the same category as the member, and who shall be appointed by the Prime Minister as a temporary member in the case of the absence or inability to act, of the member concerned.

(4) Subject to this Act, the decisions of the Commission shall be by a majority of votes and, in addition to an original vote, in any case in which the voting is equal, the Chairperson, or the person presiding at any meeting, shall have a second or casting vote.

*15 of 2008.*

(5) Minutes of every meeting of the Commission shall be kept in proper form by the Executive Director of the Commission and shall be confirmed by the Commission at its next meeting and signed by the Chairperson thereof, or by the person who presided at such meeting.

(6) The Commission may co-opt any public officer or other person to attend any particular meeting of the Commission for the purpose of assisting or advising the Commission, but no such co-opted person shall have any right to vote.

## PART III

Staff, Finances, Accounts, Reports etc., of the Commission

*Appointment of Executive Director and other staff. 15 of 2008.*

**12.** (1) The Commission shall appoint and employ, at such remuneration and subject to such terms and conditions as it thinks fit, a suitably qualified Executive Director.

(2) The Executive Director shall report to the Commission and shall, subject to the directions of the Commission, be responsible for the day-to-day operation and management of the affairs of the Commission.

(3) The Commission may appoint and employ, at such remuneration and subject to such terms and conditions as it thinks fit, such other officers and employees of the Commission as may be necessary for the proper performance of the functions and the attainment of the objectives of the Commission.

(4) The person who, immediately before the commencement of this Act, was the Director of the Commission shall continue as Executive Director as if appointed under sub-section (1).

*Duties of Exectuive Director. 15 of 2008.*

**13.** (1) Without prejudice to section 12(2), the Executive Director shall, subject to the general policy directions of the Commission, be responsible for the administration of the affairs of the Commission, including the organisation of staff in accordance with the general terms and conditions of service established by the Commission.

*15 of 2008.*

(2) The Executive Director shall have authority–

(a) to sign, jointly with the Chairperson, reports, balance sheets and other financial statements;

(b) to enter into negotiations and agreements with any organization, with the approval of the

Commission, for the attainment of the Commission’s objectives.

*Revenue of the Commission.*

**14.** The revenues of the Commission shall consist of the following–

(a) revenues allocated from the Consolidated Revenue Fund;

(b) grants or other financial assistance from international, regional or local agencies;

(c) any other monies lawfully contributed, donated, or bequeathed to the Commission from any source.

*Expenses of the Commission. 15 of 2008.*

**15.** The expenses of the Commission, including the remuneration of the Executive Director and staff, shall be paid out of its annual approved budget.

*Submission of budget estimates. 15 of 2008.*

**16.** (1) The Executive Director shall, subject to the approval of the Commission, in such form and by such date as maybe prescribed by the Financial Secretary, prepare and submit to the Minister of Finance, estimates of revenue and expenditure (including any supplementary estimates) for the Commission in respect of each financial year, and the Minister of Finance shall present the said estimates to the National Assembly with such amendments, if any, as he may consider necessary.

(2) Except with the approval of the Minister of Finance, no further sum shall be expended in any financial year other than that provided in the budget relating to such financial year.

*Accounts and audits.*

**17.** (1) The Commission shall keep accounts and other records in relation to its operations and shall prepare annually a statement of accounts in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.

(2) The Commission shall open and maintain, at such bank or banks as it may from time to time determine, accounts for the funds of the Commission.

(3) All accounts of the Commission shall be audited annually by an auditor appointed by the Commission, with the approval of the Minister of Finance.

*Annual Report.*

**18.** (1) Within four months after the end of each financial year, the Commission shall cause to be prepared and shall submit to the Prime Minister–

(a) a statement of its accounts audited in accordance with section 15 (2); and

(b) a report dealing generally with the proceedings, policies and performances of the Commission during that financial year.

(2) The Prime Minister shall cause a copy of such report, within three months of receiving it, together with the annual statement of accounts and the auditor’s report thereon, to be tabled in the National Assembly.

## PART IV

Miscellaneous

*Validity of Commission’s acts.*

**19.** No acts done or proceedings taken under this Act shall be questioned or invalidated on the grounds of–

(a) the existence of any vacancy in the membership, or of any defect in the constitution, of the Commission; or

(b) any omission, defect or irregularity.

*Exemption from income tax, property tax, stamp duty and import duty. 52 of 2017.*

**20.** (1) The Commission shall be exempt from the payment of income tax, business tax, property tax and import duty on medical supplies, medical equipment and educational material, vehicles and other equipment or supplies to fulfil the objectives of the Commission.

(2) All instruments executed by or on behalf of the Commission shall be exempt from stamp duty.

*Regulations.*

**21.** (1) The Prime Minister may make Regulations generally for the better carrying out of the objects and purposes of this Act.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, such Regulations may provide for all or any of the following–

(a) the class of activities or services, if any, for which the Commission may charge fees;

(b) the level of fees to be charged for such services.
