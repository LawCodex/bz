*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Belize Scholarship Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Education.

*Establishing of Scholarship.*

**3.** There shall be established a Scholarship to be known as the Belize Scholarship, hereinafter called “the Scholarship”.

*To be competed for in alternate years.*

**4.** The Scholarship shall be open to competition in the year 1944 and in every year thereafter.

*Defraying of costs.*

**5.** The cost of each Scholarship shall be defrayed from the Consolidated Revenue Fund.

*Regulations. 40 of 1963.*

**6.** (1) The Minister may by regulation prescribe the conditions of award, tenure and forfeiture of the Scholarship and the conditions to be observed by a person to whom the Scholarship is awarded.

(2) Regulations made under this section shall be subject to negative resolution.
